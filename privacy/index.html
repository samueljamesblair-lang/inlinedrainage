Privacy

Privacy policy

How we look after the information you share with us.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Inline Drainage is an owner-operated drainage business based in Upper Hutt. We respect your privacy and handle your personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

When you use the quote form on this website, call us or email us, we may collect:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • your suburb or area, and the service you are asking about
  • anything else you choose to tell us about the job

You don’t have to give us this information, but without it we may not be able to reply to you or prepare a quote.

Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • carry out the work and keep records of the jobs we have done

We won’t sell your information, and we won’t add you to a marketing list without asking you first.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to:

  • Your email provider and ours. The quote form on this website doesn’t send anything to us by itself. It opens a draft email in your own email app, addressed to inlinedrainage@outlook.com, and nothing is sent until you press send. Once you do, the email travels through your email provider to our mailbox, which is provided by Microsoft Outlook.
  • Councils, Wellington Water and suppliers, where a job needs it, for example for consents, connections, inspections or sign-off, or to order materials. We share only what is needed for that purpose.
  • Anyone else the law requires.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. We keep it only as long as we need it for the reasons above, or as long as the law requires.

Cookies, tracking and our website host

Our website doesn’t set cookies and doesn’t use analytics or advertising trackers. Everything on it, including fonts, scripts and images, is loaded from our own site, not from other companies.

Our website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare receives technical details such as your IP address and browser type when your browser asks for a page, so it can deliver the site and keep it secure. You can read how Cloudflare handles this in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Seeing or correcting your information

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it. To do this, or if you have any questions or concerns about your privacy, contact us:

  • inlinedrainage@outlook.com
  • Ben Carson: 027 7367 553
  • Patrick Carson: 027 2940 952

If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled a privacy concern, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use information, for example by adding analytics or a newsletter, we will update this page and the date at the top.
